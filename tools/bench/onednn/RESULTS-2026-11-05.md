## MXFP4 small-M (MTP verify, batched decode): why MXFP4 decodes slower than INT4

11-04: GRIMOIRE's Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 decoded 16-22% slower than Swift GPTQ-Int4 at the same acceptance and the same
weight bytes (13.25 vs 13.07 GiB without the embedding). Cause: MXFP4's small-M GEMM still ran from
libgrimoire_gemm.so (256 registers, its own split-K plan); INT4 moved to a 128-register build with its own plan on
10-05. Branch `mxfp4-smallm-1105`: the kernel at 128 registers in `gemv_decode.cpp`, nibble pairs (k, k+4) as INT4's
fast dequant (X reordered to match), 256-K slices (4 when K >= 12288). `tools/mx4rows_probe.cpp`, gpu0, M = 7,
