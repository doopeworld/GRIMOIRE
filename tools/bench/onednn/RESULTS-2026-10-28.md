What changed (each is exact -- the same answers, bit for bit):
1. Picking the 10 experts out of 512: a register kernel instead of a slow generic one (43 us -> 7 us a layer).
2. The output head split between the cards (each scores half the vocabulary, the best of both wins).
3. Expert kernels skip the work-groups and copies of experts that are not in this card's VRAM.
4. The stream-mix norm loads its values up front (it waited for every load in turn).
5. The expert kernels read their input from the cache instead of a shared-memory copy that put
   every thread on the same memory bank (expert kernels ~70 us -> ~45 us a layer).

One token (timeline, short context): 23.6 ms -> 19.4 ms.
